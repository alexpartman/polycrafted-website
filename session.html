Welcome. Let us begin with your room.

You now have direct access to the studio. A private session is a working conversation with Alexandre about a specific project: the space, the client, the scale, the mood. Within 24 hours of the session you receive a photoreal render of a proposed piece in that room, at true scale, before anything is committed.

What you bring

Photos or plans of the space, the palette and finishes you are working with, and a sense of the client. A phone photo of the wall is enough to start.

What you receive

A render of the piece in your room within 24 hours, your trade net price in writing, lead time, and an IP warranty on the finished work.

What the studio makes

Paintings at any scale, murals painted on site, sculpture, one-off furniture and objects. Every piece is an original, made once, for one room.
