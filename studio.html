The studio
Custom art, conceived for the space and made by hand.

The Polycrafted is a Los Angeles creative studio specialising in custom artwork for interiors. We design and produce large-scale paintings, murals, sculpture and one-off furniture and objects, each conceived for a specific space and executed by hand. Every project begins with a custom artwork consultation: we study the room, its light and its proportions, and translate your vision into a proposal you can see, rendered in place, with a fixed price and a lead time. From concept to installation, the studio takes care of everything. Whether you are an interior designer, a stager, a hotel or a private client, if you can picture it on the wall, we can make it real.

The studio

What we do

The Polycrafted makes art for specific interiors. Large-scale paintings, diamond dust on canvas, murals painted on site, sculpture, one-off furniture and objects. Every piece is designed with the room it will live in, not placed in it afterwards, and every piece is made once.

The studio is led by Alexandre Partouche, painter, working from Los Angeles for residences and projects across Southern California and beyond.

How we work

From the room to the wall

A conversation about the space

Plans or photos, the palette, the light, the client. Alexandre advises on scale, placement, medium and composition.

A proposal you can see

The concept, a photoreal render of the piece in the actual room at true scale, and a fixed price with lead time. Revised until it is right, before anything is committed.

Made by hand

The piece is painted, built or sculpted in the Los Angeles studio. Original composition, archival materials, diamond dust finish where it belongs.

Delivered and installed

Crated, delivered and hung by the studio. The room is left as it was found, with the piece on the wall.

What to expect

Original. Warranted. On time.

Every commission is an original composition, warranted as such in writing. The studio does not reproduce or reinterpret the work of named artists and does not paint celebrity likenesses. Style, palette, gesture, texture, scale and material are all open to direction.

You see the piece in your room before it exists. You receive a fixed price and a lead time before you commit. Diamond dust, the studio's signature finish, catches light from every angle and changes as you move through the room.
