Featured work

Made for one room.

Each piece starts with a space, a conversation, and a feeling. Here are a few of the rooms we have created for.

The Dining Room

Three canvases of layered hearts, sprayed and drawn over each other in pink, yellow, green, purple and white, with black outlines binding the surface together. Sized to run the length of the sideboard, with the largest set forward, so the wall reads as one piece from the table and three up close. Colour for a room of dark lacquer, gilt and plaster.

Spray and enamel on canvas, three panels
Private residence

The Living Room

Layered, translucent brush strokes in greys, whites and warm blacks over a black ground, built up until the surface reads like folded fabric or smoke. The restrained palette sits between plaster walls and rust velvet without competing; the scale fills the wall between the two sconces.

Acrylic on canvas
Private residence
02

California Modern

Broad, translucent strokes in white and grey over black, moving in a loose spiral, the strokes laid with wide brushes and knives so each pass stays visible through the next. Made for a room of warm wood, stone and rust so that the one cool element is the painting.

Acrylic on canvas
Private residence
03

Script, Two Panels

Commissioned by a designer from a reference: two long panels in the spirit of a calligraphic street style the client loved. The studio studied that visual language, its rhythm, its marks, its density, and composed something new in it. An original piece in the spirit of the reference, not a copy. Each panel is two feet by twelve on a three-inch built frame; the ground is white, off-white and beige layered for an aged surface, and every black character is painted freehand in acrylic. Inset: hand-lettered trunks for The Harrison, San Francisco.

Acrylic on stretched canvas, built frames
24 x 144 in each, 3 in deep · Private residence
04

The Harrison

A large-scale painting in gold, black, blue and white, with figures, wings and lettering, made for the living room of a penthouse with the bay behind it. The gold ground answers the gilt and marble in the room; the black holds the wall from across the space.

Mixed media on canvas
The Harrison, San Francisco
05

Kitchen View

Built for a designer's staging of a penthouse at The Harrison. The brief was a heart with real presence, so it was built rather than painted: a deep wooden frame, thick as a chocolate box, with canvas stretched over it, finished in matte black for a velvet surface. A poem runs around the entire perimeter in white hand lettering. The arrows are made by hand in wood with real feathers, painted gold and nailed into the frame.

Built wood frame, canvas, matte black, hand lettering, gilded feather arrows
The Harrison, San Francisco
06

Words

Rows of exclamations in a bold, worn sans serif, painted in silver and black over black so the words emerge and recede with the light, chosen to sit with the leather, brass and cowhide in the room. Inset: Revenge, one word broken across three lines in heavy black capitals on a white textured ground, painted large enough to fill a wall.

Text paintings on canvas
Private residences, Los Angeles
07

Figurative Works

Figures drawn in dense black line against red and pink grounds: a seated couple, a couple among flowers, two riders on a black horse. A limited palette and a strong line, made to bring a raw, expressive element into contemporary interiors.

Mixed media on canvas
Various sizes · Private residences
08

Colour, at Scale

A skull inside a burst of orange, pink, blue and green, colour flung outward from the centre, made with Premier Stagers and Max Jacquet Design at 84 by 84 inches to hold a brick wall above a curved sofa. Inset: a lion in sand tones on a dark ground, finished in diamond dust, the studio's signature layer of crushed glass that catches light from every angle, for a living room in Newport Beach.

Acrylic on canvas, 84 x 84 in · Diamond dust on canvas, 38 x 50 in
Private residences
09

Black and White

Concentric heart forms in black on black for an entry hall, made to be the first thing seen from the door: near-monochrome, so it reads as a form before it reads as an image. Inset: a large vortex in the studio, a recurring form in the studio's work, at whatever scale the wall asks for.

Mixed media on canvas
Private residence · The studio, Los Angeles
10

Objects

A heavy bag hand-painted with a portrait in black on white, with lettering and drips, so the bag reads as a piece of art when it hangs still. The studio paints on surfaces that are not canvas: leather, wood, metal, whatever the room calls for.

Painted object
The Harrison, San Francisco
