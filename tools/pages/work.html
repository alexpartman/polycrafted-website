Selected work

Made for one room.

Each piece was designed around the interior it hangs in: its light, its palette, its scale.

The Harrison, San Francisco. Built for a designer's staging of a penthouse. The brief was a heart with real presence, so it was built rather than painted: a deep wooden frame, thick as a chocolate box, with canvas stretched over it. The whole form was finished in matte black for a velvet surface, and a poem runs around the entire perimeter in white hand lettering. The arrows are made by hand in wood with real feathers, painted gold and nailed straight into the frame.

Every piece begins with a conversation about the room.
