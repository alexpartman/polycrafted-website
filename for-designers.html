The render

Your client approves the piece before it exists.

Send one photo of the wall and its dimensions. Within 24 hours you receive a photoreal render of the proposed artwork in that room, at exact scale. Palette, composition and size are revised until you sign off. Only then does a deposit change hands.

This removes the one objection that stalls commissioned art: not knowing what you are going to get. Your client sees it. You close in the same meeting.

Terms, in plain language
  1. Lead times

    Studio tier: 3 to 5 weeks from approved render. Commission tier: 6 to 10 weeks. Murals: scheduled at quote. Dates are written into the agreement.

  2. Deposit

    50 percent on signing, after the render is approved. Balance before delivery. Trade accounts in good standing can request net-30 on the balance.

  3. Revisions

    Unlimited revisions at the render stage until approval. One round of adjustments on the finished piece before delivery.

  4. Delivery and install

    Crated and installed by the studio within Los Angeles County at no charge on commission tier. Studio tier and out-of-area installs are quoted. Packaging removed, wall left clean.

  5. IP warranty

    Every piece is an original composition. The studio does not reproduce or reinterpret named artists' work or celebrity likenesses. The trade agreement warrants this and indemnifies you.

  6. Cancellation

    Before paint touches canvas, the deposit less render and design time is refundable. After production begins, the deposit is retained and the piece is completed for you.

Full terms in the trade agreement, sent with your trade account.

Questions designers ask
Can my client see the piece before we commit?

Yes. That is the whole point. One photo of the wall, and within 24 hours you have a photoreal render at true scale. Revised until approved, before any deposit.

Can you match a palette or a fabric?

Yes. Send swatches, a Pantone, or the fabric itself. Palette, gesture, texture and scale are all directed to the room.

Can you paint something "in the style of" a known artist?

No. We work in the studio's own language and can take direction on palette, energy and materials, but we do not reproduce or reinterpret a named artist's compositions or celebrity likenesses. The trade agreement warrants that every piece is original, which protects you and your client.

Do you work with staging inventories?

Yes. Studio tier is built for it: repeatable sizes, fast lead times, volume terms for stagers who buy several pieces a quarter.

Where do you install?

Los Angeles, Orange County and Beverly Hills as standard. We ship crated anywhere and can install on site by arrangement.
