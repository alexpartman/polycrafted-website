Process

Render first. Then paint.

The most expensive words in commissioned art are "I hope you like it." We removed them. You see the finished piece in your room before it exists, and you approve it before you pay anything.

A render becomes a painting. Same composition, same scale.The Polycrafted method
  1. Consultation

    Thirty minutes, by phone or on site. We talk about the room, the wall, the light, the palette you are working with, the budget tier and the date you need it by. Send a photo of the wall and its dimensions.

  2. Render in your actual room, within 24 hours

    The proposed artwork is composited into your photo at true scale. Not a sketch, not a mood board: a photoreal image of the finished piece on your wall. We revise palette, composition and size until you say yes.

  3. Approval and deposit

    You approve the render. Only then is the agreement signed and a deposit taken. Lead time is written into the agreement and starts that day.

  4. Hand-painted execution

    The piece is painted by hand in the Los Angeles studio on archival canvas with professional acrylics and oils, sealed with UV-resistant varnish. Diamond dust is applied by hand as a final layer. You receive progress photos along the way.

  5. Delivery and installation

    Crated, delivered and hung by the studio. Hardware included, packaging removed, wall left clean. Out-of-area work is shipped crated with install by arrangement.

Lead times

Studio tier 3 to 5 weeks. Commission tier 6 to 10 weeks.

From approved render. Murals scheduled at quote.

Materials

Originality

No reproductions, no reinterpretations of named artists, no celebrity likenesses. Warranted in writing.
